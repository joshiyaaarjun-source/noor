Why Noor?

Technology should feel like a safe space.

Noor is a hackathon-ready digital prototype built around one simple idea: women deserve technology that understands context, respects privacy and makes support easier to access.

  • ✓Privacy-first demo architecture. Data stays inside your browser through localStorage.
  • ✓Human-centered wellness. Tracking is designed to inform, not overwhelm.
  • ✓Mentorship that connects. Discover webinars and learning opportunities.
  • ✓Built for growth. The frontend can later connect to a secure backend and real APIs.
